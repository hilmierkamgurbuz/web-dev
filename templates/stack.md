# Stack

One line per technology decision. `chosen:` names the package with its range, and `wd check` compares it with `package.json` and the lockfile. D-### ids are shared with `decisions.md`; get the next free one with `wd id D`.

```
D-### | <area> | chosen: <pkg or service>@<range> | rejected: <alt> (<reason>), <alt> (<reason>) | why: <one line> | date: <YYYY-MM-DD> | status: active
```

Areas: framework, rendering, styling, components, database, orm, auth, validation, data-fetching, state, unit-tests, e2e-tests, lint, hosting, package-manager, monitoring, email, payments, storage, i18n.
